<objective>
Put the whole app behind one login. Better Auth (email and password, sign-up disabled, database sessions with 7-day sliding
expiry) runs on the @jio/db tables; `pnpm db:seed` creates the shared account; a guarded auth route locks the account for 15 minutes
after 5 failed attempts and writes login, login_failed, login_locked and logout events; middleware sends unauthenticated visitors
to `/login`; and `/` is an empty protected shell that logs a `view` event. Phase 04 builds the board inside this shell.
</objective>
