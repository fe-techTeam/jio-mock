  'jio #RelianceIPO' (generic hashtag as qualifier). Boundaries: 'jiomart' has no anchor; 'जियोगे' has no anchor (combining marks
  count as word characters). Entities: 'Mukesh and Akash Ambani' gives ['akash'] only (no full 'Mukesh Ambani' span);
  'Mukesh Ambani, Isha Ambani at Jio IPO' gives ['mukesh', 'isha', 'jio'], in ENTITY_KEYS order. Also check that matchedTerms holds
  the original config spelling and that compiling then matching 5,000 mixed texts takes under 2s.
  matcher.ts exports:
  - `normalizeForMatch(s: string): string`: NFKC; strip zero-width characters U+200B-U+200D and U+FEFF; lowercase; '-' and '_' become
    spaces; collapse whitespace; trim. Apply the same function to every term.
  - `type MatchResult = { relevant: boolean; reason: 'strong' | 'anchor_cooccurrence' | 'anchor_pair' | 'negative' | 'none';
    matchedTerms: string[]; entities: EntityKey[]; negativeHits: string[] }`.
  - `compileMatcher(terms: TermsConfig): (text: string) => MatchResult` and `matchTerms(text, terms)`, which caches compiled matchers
    in a WeakMap keyed by the terms object.
  Algorithm: build one regex per list, longest terms first, with escaped terms and Unicode boundaries
  `(?<![\p{L}\p{M}\p{N}])(?:…)(?![\p{L}\p{M}\p{N}])` and the flags `giu`. A '#' prefix is part of the term. Then:
  (1) find the negative spans and replace them with spaces of the same length (masking);
  (2) on the masked text, a strong hit is any ipo_terms, hashtags or entity alias match;
  (3) find anchor hits (with their group names) and qualifier hits (cooccurrence_terms plus generic_hashtags), keeping only
  qualifiers whose span does not overlap an anchor span, so '#Reliance' cannot qualify the 'Reliance' inside itself;
  (4) relevant = strong, else (any anchor and any qualifier), else (anchors from at least 2 different groups); reason follows the same order;
  (5) when nothing is relevant and negativeHits is non-empty, reason is 'negative'.
  Entities: the keys whose aliases matched, plus 'jio' when relevant through an ipo_terms or hashtags hit or through the jio anchor
  group, in ENTITY_KEYS order. Never throw on empty or very long input; truncate to 20,000 characters before matching.</action>
