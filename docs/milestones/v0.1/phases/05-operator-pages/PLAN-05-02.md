<objective>
Give operators the Settings page: edit IPO terms, hashtags, entity aliases, co-occurrence and negative terms with validation and a "Test against last 24h" match count; edit the 10 seeded topics and promote, merge or dismiss Emerging candidates; set retention days; read the audit log; and see the locked "Person-level views: Off, pending legal review" row. Every terms or retention save creates a new config version the worker picks up on its next tick.
</objective>
