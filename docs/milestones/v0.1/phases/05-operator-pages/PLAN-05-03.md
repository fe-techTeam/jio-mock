<objective>
Make labels correctable and accuracy measurable. Operators relabel any post from the evidence drawer in one click (applied at once, 10-second Undo, revert on error). On /accuracy they label a 200-post gold set one post at a time, stratified across English, Hinglish, Hindi and Gujarati. Accuracy (overall, per language, a 3x3 sentiment confusion matrix and the sarcasm subset) is computed only from gold rows against model rows, and only once 200 posts are labelled.
</objective>
